# Wordkeepers character speech

Hebrew dubbing produced on 2026-09-09 using ElevenLabs v3 through the owner's connected account: twenty-four hero encouragement clips and forty monster dialogue clips (two per encounter). Production text defaults to no Hebrew niqqud, following the owner's listening preference. The frost monster's entry line uses targeted niqqud on לְחָיַיִם (cheeks) to correct a reported pronunciation problem; displayed dialogue remains unpointed.

Hero phrases: מגניב! / מעולה! / איזו מילה! / קסם אדיר!
Added phrases: פגיעה יפה! / זה עבד! / עוד קסם מוצלח! / האותיות איתנו! / איזה כוח! / הקסם מתחזק! / ממשיכים לניצחון! / זה היה מרשים!
Boy: VALF — Child, Young, Playful & Sarcastic.
Girl: Leo 8 years girl (ElevenLabs catalogue name).
Files: assets/hero-he-{boy|girl}-{1..12}.mp3.

Hero reactions play after a valid accepted attack, never while merely selecting letters or after a rejected word. Each avatar/language uses a shuffled phrase bag: all phrases play before any repeats, with no immediate repeat across cycles. Muted or busy speech does not consume a phrase; monster dialogue keeps priority. Encounter-ending attacks omit encouragement to avoid a clipped line. The initial four clips are preloaded; additional clips load on demand.

Monster casting uses different delivery for each encounter:
- VALF Goblin: encounters 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 18.
- Kal — Ads & Audiobooks: encounters 2, 10, 17.
- VALF young hero: encounter 6.
- Leo: encounters 7, 11, 13.
- Draconius: encounters 4, 8, 12, 14, 16, 19, 20.
Files: assets/monster-he-{01..20}-{idle|hit|defeat}.mp3. Each clip corresponds to its displayed Hebrew speech bubble. Other reactions retain the game's synthesized creature effects.

On 2026-09-10, twenty unique Hebrew defeat lines were added using the same encounter cast, plus four merchant greetings using Juanjo Goblin — High-pitched Evil Minion, a male voice with a sharp, guttural timbre and raspy delivery. This is the owner's requested masculine, grating goblin direction. Files: assets/merchant-he-{01..04}.mp3, one for each shop visit after chapters 1–4. The final chapter ends in victory and has no shop. Hebrew generation text contains no niqqud.

The final merchant recordings use the male goblin voice's natural pitch and texture; they are not pitch-raised. Outer silence is trimmed with consonant padding and loudness normalized as described below. Earlier audition takes remain in the private working folder.

Defeat dialogue plays once before the result transition and is allowed to finish. Merchant greetings play when entering the shop; buying an item does not restart them, and leaving stops the voice. Muting and unavailable audio never block progression. English UI uses translated captions without Hebrew dialogue playback.

MP3s are mono at 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps. Quiet outer edges are trimmed with consonant padding; loudness targets -18 LUFS and -2 dBTP. Original takes are retained privately for review. Local transcription helps select matching takes; listening preference remains the final authority on character performance.

The eight English hero encouragement clips remain the original Microsoft online neural speech generated on 2026-09-08 using edge-tts 7.2.8: en-US-GuyNeural / en-US-AnaNeural, rate +8%, pitch +30Hz / +12Hz.
English phrases: Cool! / Excellent! / What a word! / Epic magic!
Files: assets/hero-en-{boy|girl}-{1..4}.mp3.

No player data is sent for speech. All clips ship with the game; playback needs no speech account, API key or browser voice. No PopCap audio assets are included.

## Boss comic dialogue — 2026-09-12

Five Hebrew comic lines use the existing Draconius voice, generated with ElevenLabs v3 without niqqud. Files: `assets/boss-comic-{forest,frost,mirror,ember,citadel}.mp3`. Each line plays once when the comic's third panel is revealed. Skipping or closing stops it; hidden pages pause playback, and global mute/effects volume remain authoritative. English captions do not play Hebrew speech.

These clips retain the provider MP3 output: mono, 44.1 kHz, approximately 163–170 kbps including container overhead, 3.44–4.08 seconds. Independent Scribe transcripts exactly match all five displayed lines. Decode, sizes and SHA-256 were verified; this is not a claim of human listening to every take. Generation and transcript provenance is retained privately in `docs/boss-comic-voices.json`. The four additional clips were generated sequentially after the owner explicitly requested completion; the existing forest clip is unchanged.

## Owner-supplied combat effects — 2026-09-12

The owner supplied three MP3 effects, copied byte-for-byte to stable game filenames: `effect-enemy-poof.mp3` for enemy arrival/departure smoke, `effect-life-up.mp3` for actual healing and phoenix revival, and `effect-letter-hit.mp3` once at each letter's contact with an enemy. Original files remain untouched locally; filename mapping and hashes are in `docs/provided-combat-effects.json`.

Samples are fetched and decoded ahead of combat, then independent Web Audio sources start on the existing visual impact callback, allowing overlapping hits without restarting another letter's sound. Until decoding succeeds, an immediate synthesized fallback is used; loading never triggers delayed hits. All samples follow the effects mixer, limiter, mute and pause controls. The final killing letter retains its additional explosion.

## Background music — 2026-09-10

Three MP3 tracks supplied by the project owner play in a loop: `התחלה וחנות.mp3` for shop and calm scenes, `קרבות רגילים.mp3` for ordinary battles, and `בוסים.mp3` for boss battles. The supplied audio is preserved unchanged; checksums are recorded in MUSIC_ASSETS.json in the source repository.

On 2026-09-12, the owner supplied `אותיות בראש שלי.mp3` for the opening and ending. Its game copy, `assets/opening-ending-song.mp3`, preserves the MP3 audio stream without re-encoding and removes embedded cover art and metadata: stereo, 48 kHz, 126 seconds. It automatically attempts playback and loops throughout opening menus, player setup and chapter preparation until gameplay starts or a saved adventure resumes. It starts from the beginning for one play on final campaign victory. Browser autoplay restrictions may require a first gesture; stored mute remains authoritative. The opening screen includes a music-only play/mute control. Provenance and checksums are in `docs/opening-media.json`.

Music loads on demand and follows the music volume control independently of sound effects. Recorded hero, monster and merchant dialogue lowers the music to 25% of the selected level, then restores it smoothly when speech ends. Pausing, hiding the tab or muting preserves the track position; repeated scene updates do not restart the music.
